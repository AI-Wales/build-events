# AI Wales Tech - Events

Write-ups of talks, workshops and hackathons from the AI Wales technical
community, published at [events.aiwales.org](https://events.aiwales.org).

Each page is a permanent record of what was presented, who presented it,
and where to find the code and data.

## Contributing

This site is for people who have given a live talk at any of the
AI Wales meetups in Cardiff, Swansea or Bangor. If that's you, we'd love
a write-up of your session.

1. Fork this repo.
2. Copy an existing file in `_talks/` and rename it
   `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`, using the date of your talk.
3. Fill in the front matter (title, date, location, speakers) and write
   a short summary: a couple of lines on what you covered, plus links
   to your slides, code, data and your own site.
4. Pictures would be great! Add them to `assets/images/` and reference
   them in your write-up. Please only include people who are happy to
   appear.
5. Open a pull request. We'll review it and may suggest edits before
   merging.

Prefer not to use GitHub? Send your summary and links to the organisers
and we'll put the page together for you to approve.

Once published, page URLs don't change, so you can link to your write-up
from your own site, CV or LinkedIn.
