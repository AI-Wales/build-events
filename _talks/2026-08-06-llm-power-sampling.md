The inaugural AI Wales Tech talk looked at per-request power, VRAM and
timing data for 50+ open-weight models across 21k prompts, the kind of
telemetry that is normally only visible to data centre operators and
frontier labs.

The dataset and notebooks are open on GitHub for anyone to explore,
extend or challenge via a pull request.
